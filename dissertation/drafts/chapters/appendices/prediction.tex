\chapter{Prediction}
\label{app:prediction}

The tables below report the predicted outcome for each of the 104 World Cup 2026 fixtures under both methods described in Section~\ref{sec:performance-comparison}, together with the actual result. The fixtures are grouped by tournament stage. For each match, the predicted outcome is the mode of the three-outcome distribution (home win, draw, away win) induced by the one-step-ahead score grid.
