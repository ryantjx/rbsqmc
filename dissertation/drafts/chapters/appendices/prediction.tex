\chapter{Prediction Tables}
\label{app:prediction}

This appendix collects the win--loss predictions of the bivariate-Poisson model under the EKF and RB-SQMC methods described in Section~\ref{sec:performance-comparison}. For each match, the predicted outcome is the mode of the three-outcome distribution (home win, draw, away win) induced by the one-step-ahead score grid, computed before the known result is assimilated.

\section{World Cup 2026 fixtures}
\label{app:prediction-fixtures}

Table~\ref{tab:prediction-appendix} reports the predicted outcome for each of the four World Cup 2026 fixtures under both methods, together with the actual result.

\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\begin{tabular}{llccc}
\toprule
Home team & Away team & Actual result & EKF predicted & RB-SQMC predicted \\
\midrule
Spain & Belgium & $2$--$1$ & Away win & Home win \\
Norway & England & $1$--$2$ & Home win & Away win \\
Argentina & Switzerland & $3$--$1$ & Away win & Home win \\
France & Spain & $0$--$2$ & Home win & Away win \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{Win--loss predictions of the bivariate-Poisson model for the World Cup 2026 fixtures. The predicted outcome is the mode of the three-outcome distribution under each method.}
\label{tab:prediction-appendix}
\end{table}
