\chapter{Prediction Tables}
\label{app:prediction}

This appendix collects the win--loss predictions of the bivariate-Poisson model under the EKF and RB-SQMC methods described in Section~\ref{sec:performance-comparison}. For each match, the predicted outcome is the mode of the three-outcome distribution (home win, draw, away win) induced by the one-step-ahead score grid, computed before the known result is assimilated.
