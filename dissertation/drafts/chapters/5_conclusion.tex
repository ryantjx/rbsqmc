\section{Conclusion}
We developed the framework for performing SQMC on parallel accelerators in JAX. We performed a detailed simulation study to evaluate the performance of SQMC operations between GPU and CPU. We then applied SQMC to a football model and demonstrated that SQMC can be used to perform inference on a high-dimensional problem. Although the model was not successful qualitatively, we believe the methodology provides a future framwork on tackling high-dimensional problems.
