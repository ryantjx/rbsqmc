# ST955 Masters Dissertation - Classification Framing Checklist

*Last updated Nov-24*

The following criteria can be apply to all dissertations, as appropriate:

## GENERAL

| Questions | Bad Fail (0%-39%) | Fail (40%-49%) | Pass (50%-59%) | Merit (60%-69%) | Distinction (70%-100%) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Is the material organised into sections and appendices, with appropriate content for each? | The material is badly organised and lacks features such as an appropriate introduction, a conclusion, a table of contents, a bibliography. | The material is poorly organised and has only a passable introduction, conclusion and table of contents. | The material is adequately organised into sections and appendices, with appropriate introduction, conclusion, and table of contents. | The material is quite well organised into sections and appendices, with appropriate introduction, conclusion, and table of contents. | The material is well organised into appropriate sections and appendices. |
| Is the choice and use of notation suitable? | The notation is inadequate. | The notation used is barely adequate. | The notation chosen is suitable, but shows some major inconsistencies. | The notation is reasonably fitting, but shows minor inconsistencies. | The notation is well chosen and used fittingly. |
| Are diagrams, graphs and tables appropriate and well presented? | Diagrams, graphs and tables are badly drawn or badly presented. | Diagrams, graphs and tables are poorly drawn or presented. | Diagrams, graphs and tables are presented adequately. | Diagrams, graphs and tables are presented well, but not outstandingly so. | The diagrams, graphs and tables are well chosen and used fittingly. |
| Is the bibliography complete and with citations properly made? | The bibliography is seriously lacking and citations are used incorrectly. | The bibliography is inadequate and citations have been missed or are inappropriate. | There are omissions from the bibliography, and some citations are missing. | A reasonably sufficient bibliography is supplied and most citations are properly made. | A full bibliography is supplied and citations are properly made. |
| Is the grammar, spelling and typography correct? | There are serious lapses in grammar, spelling and typography. | Grammar, spelling and typography show frequent lapses, some major. | Grammar, spelling and typography show occasional lapses, some major. | Grammar, spelling and typography show occasional minor lapses. | Grammar, spelling and typography are correct. |
| Is there evidence of significant insight and original thought in communicating the critical issues? | Serious misunderstanding of key concepts and issues. | Little evidence of insight and ideas tend to be highly derivative. | Occasional evidence of insight into the issues underlying the dissertation, but little evidence of original thinking. | Reasonable insight and some evidence of original thought in dealing with the critical issues. | Evidence of significant insight and original thought in dealing with the critical issues. |
| Is the flow of the material well-structured and logically argued? | The work is very poorly written and shows a serious inability to structure and present a logical argument. | The work is not well written and shows flaws in the structuring of logical arguments. | Generally competently written, although some problems exist in the logical organisation of the text and the way it is expressed. | The dissertation is well written, logically argued and generally well structured. | Excellent command of expression and logical argument in an adroitly structured dissertation. |
| Is there wide coverage of relevant literature?Is there superior evaluation and integration of relevant literature? | Coverage of the necessary literature is inadequate, with little information provided relevant to the claims made, or conclusions drawn, within the dissertation. | Coverage of the necessary literature is weak, with insufficient information provided to support the arguments made, or conclusions drawn, within the dissertation. | Provides an adequate coverage of the literature, although it tends to be more descriptive than evaluative, and arguments are often disjointed. | The evaluation and integration of the existing literature is very sound without being excellent. | Superior evaluation and integration of existing literature. |

## THEORETICAL PROJECTS

| Questions | Bad Fail (0%-39%) | Fail (40%-49%) | Pass (50%-59%) | Merit (60%-69%) | Distinction (70%-100%) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Is there evidence of mastery of new material at least at an Masters level? | No evidence that the candidate has mastered new material at the level of Masters. | Little evidence that the candidate has mastered new material at the level of Masters. | Evidence that the candidate has mastered some new material, generally at the level of Masters. | Evidence that the candidate has mastered substantial new material, generally at the level of Masters. | Evidence that there has been mastery of substantial new material at or beyond the level of Masters. Evidence of originality in terms of new insights into a possibly well-established area. |
| Does the material form a model for an Masters course comprising the subject of the material? | The dissertation constitutes an inadequate set of notes for an Masters course comprising the subject of the material. | The dissertation constitutes a poor set of notes for an Masters course comprising the subject of the material. | The dissertation constitutes a reasonably good set of notes for an Masters course comprising the subject of the material. | The dissertation could, with some corrections, form a model for a Masters course comprising the subject of the material (for instance, a Topics course). | The dissertation forms a model for an Masters course comprising the subject of the material (for instance, a Topics course). |
| Is there a good choice of illustrative examples? | Few or no appropriate illustrative examples. | Few appropriate illustrative examples. | Illustrative examples are well-known, or not entirely appropriate. | Good choice of illustrative examples. | Excellent choice of illustrative examples. |
| Is there insightful and pertinent analysis of novel examples? | Poor analysis of examples, with serious errors or misconceptions. | Poor analysis of examples, with frequent errors or misconceptions. | Some good analysis of examples, but with occasional errors or misconceptions. | Clear but prosaic analysis of novel examples, or imaginative analysis of standard examples. | Insightful and pertinent analysis of novel examples. |

## APPLIED PROJECTS

| Questions | Bad Fail (0%-39%) | Fail (40%-49%) | Pass (50%-59%) | Merit (60%-69%) | Distinction (70%-100%) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| In accessing data, did the student have to collect or  source the data? | No fail on this questions is possible. It is not essential for students to collect or source data. | Data accessed poorly related to substantive question, and questions not revised | Adequate data accessed, with limited discussion of context, advantages and disadvantages. | Sensible data accessed, with sensible discussion of context, advantages and disadvantages. | Insightful and appropriate choice of data, with good reporting of choices and challenges |
| Did the data require cleaning, wrangling or merging? | No evidence of preparation of data, or naive use of complete case analysis | Serious flaws exist in the preparation of data. | Attention to data merging and cleaning is generally adequate but is marred by errors and oversights. | Careful attention to data merging and cleaning, although possibly containing minor but retrievable errors. | Evidence of careful attention to critical data quality issues, such as errors, missing data, and accuracy of matching |
| In executing applied aspects of the work, has there been careful attention to critical design issues? | Serious flaws exist in the applied aspects of the dissertation making it difficult or impossible for the dissertation to meet its aims. | Serious flaws exist in the attention to the applied aspects of the dissertation, making it difficult for the dissertation to meet its aims. | Attention to the applied aspects of the dissertation is generally adequate but is marred by errors and oversights. | Adequate design for the applied aspects of the dissertation, although possibly containing minor but retrievable errors. | Evidence of careful attention to critical design issues in the execution of applied aspects of the dissertation. |
| Is the choice of data analysis appropriate? | Data analysis techniques are inappropriate and the results are presented inadequately. | Data analysis techniques are arbitrary or inappropriate. | Serviceable choice of data analysis, although other approaches may have been more appropriate. | Choice of data analysis that is mostly appropriate for the design, with clear presentation of results. | Insightful and appropriate choice of data analysis and excellent presentation and reporting of results. |
| Is the application of methodology not taught (new methodology)  to new data correct? | Incorrect or inappropriate application of new methodology to new data. | The choice or implementation of new methodology to new data is seriously flawed | The choice or implementation of new methodology to new data has some merits but is problematic. | Generally correct and appropriate application of new methodology to new data. | Correct and appropriate application of new methodology to new data. |
| Is the application of methodology from a taught module (known methodology)  to a novel area of application or new data correct? | Incorrect or inappropriate application of known methodology to new data. | The choice or implementation of known methodology to new data is seriously flawed | The choice or implementation of known methodology to new data has some merits but is problematic. | Generally correct and appropriate application of known methodology to . | Correct and appropriate application of known methodology to new data. |
| Is the presentation and reporting of results appropriate? | The presentation of reults is incoherent. | The results are poorly presented. | The presentation of results lacks clarity. | The presentation of results is mostly clear. | The presentation of results is clear and coherent. |
| Is there clear and coherent interpretation of data and the results of other studies? | Serious misinterpretations of results. | Interpretations are superficial, demonstrating a weak understanding of the results and their relevance to the theoretical framework. | Interpretation of results or other studies is adequate but limited. | Generally sound but pedestrian interpretation of results and their importance to the theoretical context. | Clear and coherent interpretation of the data, and/or the results of other studies. |
| Is there comprehensive understanding of the importance of the results in the context of the theoretical framework? | An inability to show how the results of the dissertation relate to the theoretical framework. | Not sufficient evidence of understanding of the importance of the results in the context of the theoretical framework. | Partial understanding of the importance of the results in the context of the theoretical framework. | Good understanding of the importance of the results in the context of the theoretical framework. | Comprehensive understanding of the importance of the results in the context of the theoretical framework. |

## OVERALL

| Questions | Bad Fail (0%-39%) | Fail (40%-49%) | Pass (50%-59%) | Merit (60%-69%) | Distinction (70%-100%) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  | Insuffient understanding of material at Masters level. Exposition of results is has serious flaws. Incoherent presentation. | Limited understanding of material at Masters level. Exposition/analysis of results contains serious mistakes. Presentation of results is mostly incoherent. | Some understanding of material at Masters level. Exposition/analysis of results is somewhat problematic. Presentation of results is not always coherent or well structured. | Good understanding of material at Masters level and sufficiently detailed exposition of backgrount material. Coherent, well organised and logical presentation. | Mastery of material which is beyond Masters level, and evidence of originality along with a sufficiently detailed exposition of both background material and the original contribution. Note that a dissertation which either does not display originality, or is at a technical level which can be found elsewhere at Masters level, then even if flawless will not ordinarily achieve a strong first class mark (80+). |

| 70%-79% | 80%-89% | 90%-100% |

| The student shows a high degree of competence in all criteria, but does not meet the requirements for a strong first. At this level a student may have tackled material of a high level of difficulty, demonstrated a solid understanding of the material and established possible research directions / made some original contribution which has not been fully developed. | The student shows excellence in most assessment criteria and a high degree of competence in others, mastery of some advanced material, and clearly demonstrates that there is both a detailed knowledge of the existing literature and an original contribution in either approach or interpretation. The written dissertation has substance which is close to publishable quality. | The student shows excellence in all assessment criteria, evident mastery of advanced material beyond Masters level, and clearly demonstrates that there is both an advanced knowledge and understanding of the existing literature AND a substantive (non-derivative) original contribution. The dissertation is an authoritative work which, with minor stylistic and presentational alteration, would be of publication quality in an appropriate journal. |

## OWNERSHIP

| Questions | Bad Fail (0%-39%) | Fail (40%-49%) | Pass (50%-59%) | Merit (60%-69%) | Distinction (70%-100%) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Was there little technical help from the topic supervisor, relative to the difficulty of the material? | The candidate largely ignored advice and guidance, provided by the supervisor, that might have raised the level of achievement. | Relative to the difficulty of the material, the candidate required very substantial help and guidance from the supervisor. | Relative to the difficulty of the material, the candidate required substantial technical help and guidance from the supervisor. | Relative to the difficulty of the material, the candidate required some technical help from the project supervisor. | Relative to the difficulty of the material, the candidate required little technical help from the project supervisor. |
| Was the student pro-actively engaged in the formulation and development of the dissertation projects? | The student had no input in the development of the core ideas or research direction and was not able to follow along. | The student had no input in the development of the core ideas or research direction but was able to follow along. | The student had limited role in the development of the core ideas or research direction. | The student had some role in the development of the core ideas or research direction. | The student had a key role in the development of the core ideas and the research direction of the project. |
